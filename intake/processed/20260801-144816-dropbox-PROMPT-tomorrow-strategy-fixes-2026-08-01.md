## 3. Theme common-shock engine (correlated regulatory outcomes) — build in full
- Reality this encodes: regulatory outcomes correlate by THEME — one rule change, enforcement initiative, or court decision hits an entire cohort simultaneously. Independent-event or Gaussian-correlation pricing understates bundle tail risk (the judgment-preservation-insurance blowups were exactly correlated-legal-outcome mispricing). CDS-index intuition understates it too.
- Factor taxonomy: extend contract_factor_loadings into a regulatory-theme factor model — jurisdiction × agency × rule-family × industry × trigger-class. Every contract, loan-embedded exposure, and bundle receives loadings at generation time; hivemind conviction signals feed factor-level (not just name-level) probabilities.
- Bundle construction: diversification score + per-THEME concentration caps (no theme > X% of bundle expected loss, X in live_model_config) in addition to per-name caps; the generator must reject or rebalance theme-concentrated bundles and record the test in synth_characterization_receipts.
- Pricing: common-shock premium via correlated Monte Carlo with tail dependence (t-copula or regime-switching common-factor model — NOT independence, NOT Gaussian copula); systemic scenario catalog: administration change, omnibus rulemaking, coordinated state-AG action, circuit split, enforcement-initiative wave, agency shutdown/defunding; backtests against historical regulatory shock episodes.
- Risk rails: theme-factor VaR in the greeks/var pipeline; tail_risk_alerts wired to theme concentration + velocity; theme circuit breakers that pause NEW same-theme exposure on spikes (never force-close existing positions).
- Product: standing THEME-INDEX overlays (macro regulatory indices: aggregate, objectively-settled, third-party-verifiable triggers, no identifiable-individual-decision underliers) so counterparties can hedge/trade the systemic leg separately from idiosyncratic bundles. Overlay quoting uses §2 predicted-IOI semantics.
