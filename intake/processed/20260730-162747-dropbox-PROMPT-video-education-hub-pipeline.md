# Video Education Hub + Pipeline (apparently.com, apparentlylaw.com, tomorrow, pareto)

Operator directive 2026-07-30. Brian has recorded general gaming-law videos; more are coming.
Build the display surfaces + the intake pipeline + social distribution. Reference aesthetic:
norm.law's video presentation (beautiful, editorial, trust-building) — match or beat it.

## Surfaces
1. apparentlylaw.com: a first-class "Learn" hub — featured video hero, topic rails (sweepstakes,
   licensing, AML, promos), per-video pages with transcript, key-takeaway chips, related verdict-
   card/report links, attorney attribution (Brian, per the 5.8b disclosure rules on every page),
   and the standing not-legal-advice banner.
2. apparently.com: video modules embedded contextually — a licensing page shows the licensing
   video; the learn/index gains a video rail; the newsletter embeds the week's video.
3. tomorrow + pareto: same component family, their idioms (tomorrow: hedging/market explainers;
   pareto: personal-finance explainers), slots ready even before content exists.

## Pipeline (drop a raw video -> published everywhere)
Intake (upload/drive-link) -> auto-transcribe -> AI edit pass (chapters, title/description
variants, key-takeaway extraction, thumbnail candidates, caption file) -> compliance pass (the
same commission gate class: attorney-content requires Brian's 1-click sign-off; disclosure
overlays baked in) -> publish to site hub(s) -> derivative cuts (60-90s verticals per chapter)
queued for social (YouTube, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, Instagram) with per-platform copy -> performance
loop (views/engagement per topic feed the content-focus decisions and the newsletter).

## 50-500X hooks
- Every video auto-links to its supporting verdict cards/reports ("watch, then read the cited
  authority") — video as the top of the credibility funnel into the living library.
- Transcript chunks enter the corpus as SOURCE material (attributed, freshness-tracked) so the
  advisors can cite "as Brian explains in [video, 2:14]".
- Auto-generated chapter pages = SEO surface per question ("Is a sweepstakes model legal in
  Texas?" chapter ranks on the exact query).
- "Ask about this video": the chat prompt pre-loaded with the transcript context.
- Hosting: stream via a paid-tier provider (Mux/Cloudflare Stream/YouTube-embed fallback) —
  never self-host video bytes (same zero-copy cost rule as ingest).
