## 9c. ECP-partner product studio — easy client-product setup (operator round 4; strategy 6.4 — READ IT for the mental model)
- Retail can't be a swap counterparty and can't be sold premium-for-payout (insurance). So protection reaches them as a banking/credit feature, in TWO buckets by why the user is there:

  (1) Rate-protected loan (a mortgage/SMB/auto loan they needed anyway + embedded cap → "your rate is capped");
  (2) Revenue-linked forbearance loan (working-capital financing they needed anyway; payments auto-pause/reduce on an objective sector index drop).
- BUCKET 2 — pure protection, no borrowing need → the STANDBY PROTECTION LINE (this is the key clarification): a committed contingent credit line that only DRAWS if the parametric trigger fires; the client pays a small STANDBY/COMMITMENT FEE on the undrawn line = the premium equivalent, but it's a bona-fide loan-commitment fee on a bona-fide contingent facility (NOT insurance). No event → line expires (they paid the standby fee). Event fires → instant pre-committed liquidity at pre-agreed terms exactly when they need cash = the protection, delivered as credit not indemnity. Swap sits between lender and platform capacity. Instances: key-person standby line, licensing/enforcement legal-defense standby line, rate-spike standby, revenue-collapse standby.
  - Escrow variant (operator's intuition): lender parks the payout in escrow, client pays "interest" on it = premium. Works, more intuitive, but ties up capital — offer as a premium "fully-funded" tier; committed-standby is the capital-efficient default (how real contingent-capital facilities work).
- INVESTMENT side (not a loan): Principal-protected note / structured deposit (principal + index upside, downside hedged by a parametric floor) — sources the yield/SUPPLY side of the book.
- Setup ease: partner picks a product → sets client-facing terms → platform auto-structures the embedded swap, prices it (marginal-risk + capacity), papers it, PRE-PROVISIONS for not-yet-acquired clients (publish an offer; clients onboard on demand with pool capacity pre-reserved). Aggregation (9b) nets many small products into efficient ECP-level exposure; loans/lines create hedge demand, notes create hedge supply — balance both from the same partner relationships.
